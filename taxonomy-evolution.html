2010
Brignull — Foundational Taxonomy
Coined "dark patterns." 12 types including Trick Questions, Roach Motel, Confirmshaming, Forced Continuity. Focused on web UI deception.
foundational
2016
Bosch et al. — Privacy Dark Strategies
8 dark strategies antagonizing privacy-by-design: Maximize, Publish, Centralize, Preserve, Obscure, Deny, Violate, Fake.
privacy
2018
Gray et al. — 5 Strategy Categories
Landmark HCI consolidation: Nagging, Obstruction, Sneaking, Interface Interference, Forced Action. Became the dominant organizing framework.
HCI landmark
2019
Mathur et al. — Effect-Based Classification
Empirical study of 11,000 shopping sites. 5 effect-based categories: Asymmetric, Covert, Deceptive, Obstructive, Restrictive. Cross-references cognitive biases.
empirical
2022
EDPB / FTC / OECD — Regulatory Frameworks
Multiple regulatory bodies codify dark pattern categories. EDPB: Overloading, Skipping, Hindering, Fickle, Left in the Dark. FTC: 7 categories.
regulatory
2024
Gray et al. — 3-Level Ontology (CHI '24)
Harmonized 10 taxonomies into 65 patterns across 3 levels: 5 high-level strategies → 25 meso-level approaches → 35 low-level instantiations.
ontology
2024
Li et al. — 68-Type DPAF Framework
Extended Gray's ontology with 24 new patterns and 8 new subcategories from 113-paper systematic review. Adds severity ratings and scenario mapping.
comprehensive
2025
DarkBench (Kran) & DarkPatterns-LLM (Asif)
First AI-native taxonomies. DarkBench: 6 LLM categories (sycophancy, anthropomorphism, brand bias). Asif: 7 harm types with 401 curated examples. Shift from interface to content.
AI-native
2025
Kitkowska — Hyper-Dark Patterns
Theorizes AI-personalized dark patterns that dynamically adapt to individual behavioral profiles. Traditional categories + real-time vulnerability targeting.
AI-personalized
