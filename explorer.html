Clustered Pattern Density

Rectangle area = number of low-level pattern instances within each cluster. Click any rectangle to zoom in; click the breadcrumb or background to zoom out. Color encodes the high-level strategy.

Interaction: Click a colored block to drill into that cluster. The breadcrumb trail tracks your path. Use "Reset Zoom" or click the root breadcrumb to return to the top level. Hover for pattern details and cross-taxonomy references.

Hierarchical Ontology — By Mechanism

Three concentric rings: inner = high-level strategy, middle = meso-level approach, outer = low-level instantiation. Click any arc to zoom into that branch. Click the center to zoom back out.

Interaction: Click an arc to focus on that branch. The center circle always zooms you back one level. Hover for details. Arc width = number of descendant patterns. The full Gray et al. (2024) + Li et al. (2024) ontology is represented.
